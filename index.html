Our story

Three Generations.
One Timeless Tradition.

Sri Bikrampur Bastralaya is a family saree business rooted in tradition, craftsmanship and the timeless elegance of Indian textiles.

Across three generations, our love for sarees and appreciation for their beauty has been a part of our family journey. We believe every saree carries a story, a tradition and a special memory waiting to be created.

Today, we continue this legacy with the same love for traditional elegance, bringing sarees closer to generations of customers.
